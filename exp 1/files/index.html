About the System

The AI-based Smart Irrigation Advisory System helps sugarcane farmers make data-driven irrigation decisions using real-time soil moisture, weather, and crop-stage information collected through IoT sensors.

What This Portal Offers

Farmer registration, irrigation recommendations, live weather data, crop monitoring dashboards, and multimedia advisory content for farmers, agricultural officers, researchers, and sugar factory management.
